AI voice calls for what
chat can't reach.

Some people never read messages and always pick up the phone. For them there's an AI voice that calls, says what it's calling about, and writes down the answer. It doesn't replace the chat agent: it sits on top of the plan you already have.

Book your consultation Ask IntelliA

What it does, exactly

Voice is for specific moments, not for replacing your sales team. These are the ones it handles well:

  • Confirming an appointment the day before, and recording whether the person confirms, moves it or cancels.
  • Reminding someone of a booked visit, with the time and the place.
  • Following up with someone who left their details and never wrote again.
  • Leaving the call in writing in the same panel as everything else: the call doesn't live only in the memory of whoever made it.

How it's done

Scope and minutes are agreed in the consultation, because the cost depends on volume. You won't find a per-minute rate here: the one that fits you comes from knowing how many calls you'll actually make.

  1. What for. We decide which calls get automated and which stay human.
  2. The script. What the voice says, what it asks, and at what point it hands the call to a person.
  3. The hook-up. It's connected to the agent and the calendar you already have, so the call knows which appointment it's talking about.
  4. Pilot. A small group first; we listen to the recordings and fix things before opening it up.

What you need to provide

  • An agent plan with us already running: voice sits on top, it isn't sold on its own.
  • A rough idea of volume: how many calls a month.
  • A decision on what should happen when the person asks to speak to a real human.

What it doesn't do

Saying it here is cheaper than finding it out in the third meeting.

  • No cold outreach and no calling bought lists.
  • It doesn't close sales. It confirms, reminds and follows up; the rest is your team.
  • There's no published per-minute rate: it depends on volume and is quoted in the consultation.
  • It isn't sold without an agent: it's an add-on to the plan, not a separate product.
