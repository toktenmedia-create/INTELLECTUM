What it does, exactly

It's a one-off payment, and the first month of the reception assistant is included. If you also want WhatsApp, the matching agent plan is added on top.

  • Genuinely fast: speed is the first thing Google looks at and the first thing anyone on a phone notices.
  • With the agent inside from launch, not bolted on afterwards.
  • Measurable: measurement is installed so you know where people arrive from and what they do, instead of spending on ads blind.
  • Ready for search engines: clean URLs, structured data, a sitemap, and copy written for people.
  • Yours: the domain is registered in your name and the site is handed over to you.

How it's done

If you already have a site, it doesn't have to go: we keep it and integrate the AI on top. Building from scratch is only for people who don't have one or want to change it.

  1. What it has to achieve. Before any talk of design: what the page is for and what should happen when someone lands on it.
  2. Structure and copy. We decide what goes in each section and write it; the copy is half the work.
  3. Build. Design, development, and the agent connected.
  4. Launch and measurement. It goes live, gets measured, and is corrected with real data.

What you need to provide

  • Your logo and your photos, if you have them; if not, we sort it out.
  • Clarity on what you sell and to whom: without that, no copy works.
  • The domain, registered in your name and paid by you, so the site is genuinely yours.

What it doesn't do

Saying it here is cheaper than finding it out in the third meeting.

  • No templates filled in with your logo.
  • We don't promise a position on Google: we do what it takes to compete, and Google decides the position.
  • Hosting and the domain in later years are on you.
